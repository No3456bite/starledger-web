# Account icon sources

The account picker and account page use the same catalog. For nine verified apps, the colored artwork is loaded from Apple's App Store image CDN; if it cannot load, the bundled local mark stays visible. The website sends no ledger data to the image CDN. Bank marks and other fallback marks remain bundled with the static site.

- WeChat, Alipay, Meituan, Pinduoduo, Douyin, JD, Dewu, UnionPay App / 云闪付, and Digital Yuan: App Store CN artwork URLs recorded in [HQ ICON](https://github.com/iosyyds/hq-icon)'s 2026-11-01 data, checked against the app name and publisher. The image files belong to their respective app publishers; the MIT license of the search tool does not license the artwork. The URLs are a dated snapshot and can change when apps update.

- WeChat, Alipay, Meituan, PayPal, Apple and Apple Pay local marks: [Simple Icons 16.36.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 1.0. These brand marks appear on colored tiles when no App Store artwork is available. The tiles are not copies of the official app icons. Brand names and marks remain the property of their owners.
- Chinese bank marks: [dingyong0214/bank-logo](https://github.com/dingyong0214/bank-logo), original 120 × 120 PNG files. The source repository does not publish an explicit license for the images; these are used as third-party identifiers, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.
- Pinduoduo, Digital Yuan, UnionPay App / 云闪付, JD and Dewu fallback marks: [Arcticons](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons), via [Iconify Arcticons 1.2.59](https://icon-sets.iconify.design/arcticons/), CC BY-SA 4.0. The icon IDs are `pinduoduo`, `digital-yuan`, `unionpay-app`, `jd-com` and `dewu`. These are community drawn outlines, not official artwork. Their SVG strokes were made thicker to remain legible at the small account picker size; the colored tile is added by this project. The modified SVGs remain under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Douyin note mark: ByteDance’s [Semi Icons 2.104.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@douyinfe/semi-icons), MIT, `tiktok_logo.svg`. This is the shared note silhouette, not the colored Douyin app badge.

The bank set covers ICBC, ABC, BOC, CCB, Bank of Communications, PSBC, CMB, SPDB, CITIC, CEB, CMBC, CIB, Ping An Bank, CGB, Huaxia Bank, Bank of Beijing, Bank of Shanghai, Bank of Jiangsu, Bank of Ningbo, Bank of Nanjing, and WeBank.

MYbank / 网商银行 has no verified asset and keeps the ordinary account icon or a user-selected Emoji. The collection search also checked [SuperTinyIcons](https://github.com/edent/SuperTinyIcons), [Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons) and [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons); none supplied a suitable complete set of these Chinese app icons. The app sends only image requests to Apple's image CDN for the verified artwork; the local fallback marks are bundled with the site.
