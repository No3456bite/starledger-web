# Account icon sources

The account picker and account page use the same local icon catalog. Icons are bundled with the static site so selecting an account does not contact an icon service.

- WeChat, Alipay, Meituan, PayPal, Apple and Apple Pay: [Simple Icons 16.36.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 1.0. Their brand marks are shown on colored tiles for legibility; the tiles are not copies of the official app icons. Brand names and marks remain the property of their owners.
- Chinese bank marks: [dingyong0214/bank-logo](https://github.com/dingyong0214/bank-logo), original 120 × 120 PNG files. The source repository does not publish an explicit license for the images; these are used as third-party identifiers, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.
- Pinduoduo, Digital Yuan and UnionPay App / 云闪付: [Arcticons](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons), via [Iconify Arcticons 1.2.59](https://icon-sets.iconify.design/arcticons/), CC BY-SA 4.0. The respective icon IDs are `pinduoduo`, `digital-yuan` and `unionpay-app`. These are community drawn outlines of the apps, not the official artwork. Their SVG strokes were made thicker to remain legible at the small account picker size; the colored tile is added by this project. The modified SVGs remain under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Douyin note mark: ByteDance’s [Semi Icons 2.104.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@douyinfe/semi-icons), MIT, `tiktok_logo.svg`. This is the shared note silhouette, not the colored Douyin app badge.

The bank set covers ICBC, ABC, BOC, CCB, Bank of Communications, PSBC, CMB, SPDB, CITIC, CEB, CMBC, CIB, Ping An Bank, CGB, Huaxia Bank, Bank of Beijing, Bank of Shanghai, Bank of Jiangsu, Bank of Ningbo, Bank of Nanjing, and WeBank.

MYbank / 网商银行 has no verified asset and keeps the ordinary account icon or a user-selected Emoji. The collection search also checked [SuperTinyIcons](https://github.com/edent/SuperTinyIcons), [Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons) and [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons); none supplied a suitable complete set of these Chinese app icons. Account icon choices are bundled locally and do not make requests to the source projects.
